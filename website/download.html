What happens after you double-click it

01
A console window opens
It scans your CPU, RAM, and GPU directly - nothing is uploaded anywhere.
02
You get a plain-language result
How many models you can run comfortably, with offload, or not at all - right there in the console.
03
The dashboard opens automatically
Your default browser opens to the full report at 127.0.0.1 - local only, nothing leaves your machine.
🔒 CanIRunLLM does not upload your hardware information anywhere by default. The scan, the model evaluation, and the dashboard all run on your machine.
